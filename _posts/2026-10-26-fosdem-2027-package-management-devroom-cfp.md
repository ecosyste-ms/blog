The Package Management devroom returns to FOSDEM on **Sunday, 31 January 2027, from 13:15 to 17:00** at the Université libre de Bruxelles, Belgium. The call for proposals is open, with a **submission deadline of 1 December 2026 at 23:59 UTC**.

Wolf Vollprecht and Andrew Nesbitt organised the 2026 devroom after [last year's call for participation]({% post_url 2025-11-06-fosdem-2026-package-managers-devroom-cfp %}) on this blog. The [nine-talk programme]({% post_url 2025-12-20-fosdem-2026-package-managers-devroom-schedule %}) included Matthew Suozzo on inspecting package builds with OSS Rebuild and Ryan Gibb on modelling dependency resolution across package managers.

## About the devroom

We welcome talks from maintainers, researchers, and users of package managers, whether you work on a Linux distribution, a language registry, HPC packaging, or formats such as Flatpak. Topics include dependency resolution, reproducibility, namespace collisions, phantom dependencies, metadata standards, dependencies across ecosystems, and artifact signing.

This year's [CFP on the Software Stewardship Lab blog](https://stewardshiplab.org/ideas/cfp-package-management-devroom-at-fosdem-27/) also invites discussion of growing package submissions due to LLM usage and new supply chain attacks. We'd like to hear how maintainers are handling these alongside funding shortages and changes in governance.

## Submitting a talk

Talks should have a substantial technical focus on package management and software infrastructure. We particularly welcome topics that receive less attention elsewhere, and proposals from people and communities underrepresented in free and open source software. Frequently covered topics, such as adding AI to existing security approaches, will receive lower priority. Talks primarily promoting products will not be accepted.

[Submit your proposal through FOSDEM's pretalx system](https://pretalx.fosdem.org/fosdem-2027/cfp), selecting **"Package Management"** as the track. We suggest including a brief biography for each speaker and links to relevant previous work. Talks are **20 minutes** by default. If you would prefer a **10-minute** slot, request it in the "Submission Notes" field and the organisers should be able to accommodate it.

- 25 October 2026: call for proposals opens
- 1 December 2026, 23:59 UTC: submissions close
- 4 December 2026: selected speakers notified
- 7 December 2026: schedule published

Submitting a proposal means agreeing to the [FOSDEM Code of Conduct](https://fosdem.org/2027/practical/conduct/). The devroom is organised by Andrew Nesbitt and Vlad Miricioiu (Software Stewardship Lab), Wolf Vollprecht (prefix.dev), and Travis Hathaway (Anaconda). For questions about the devroom or a submission, email [package-management-devroom-manager@fosdem.org](mailto:package-management-devroom-manager@fosdem.org).
